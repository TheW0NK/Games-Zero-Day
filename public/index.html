Getting Started

Welcome to Zero Day

A quick reference — reopen this anytime from the Start Menu.

Desktop basics

Click any icon on the desktop or in the taskbar to open it. Click a window to bring it to front. Right-click almost anything — desktop, files, windows, the taskbar — for more options.

Money

Everyone starts with $2,500. Open Bank to see your balance, send money, or check the transaction history. In the Terminal: wallet shows your balance, transfer <user> <amount> sends money, leaderboard shows the richest accounts. Playing Snake or 2048 also pays out a small bonus based on your score.

Hacking another player

Everything below happens in the Terminal:

1. scan <user> — free recon, shows their open ports.

2. exploit <user> <port> <bruteforce|stealth|injection> ($20) — starts a code-breaking puzzle instead of an instant win. guess n n n n (four numbers, 1-6 each) to crack it — you'll get exact/partial feedback like Mastermind. puzzle shows where you left off.

3. Crack it and you're in for 2 minutes — steal takes a cut of their balance, or deploy <malware> installs something nastier (see malware for the full list).

Payouts scale with how much security a target has invested — a completely undefended account (0% security) has nothing worth stealing, so there's no money in picking on brand-new players.

Defending yourself

secure <port> <ratelimiter|sentinel|decoy> ($150) installs a defense module — each one beats one attack approach and loses to another, so mixing them up makes you harder to read. firewall upgrade / antivirus upgrade give a smaller general edge. avscan cleans off infections. The Firewall and Antivirus apps do the same thing with buttons instead of commands.

If someone breaches you, you'll get a real-time alert with a short window to type counter and try to expel them before they act.

Chat

The Chat app has three tabs: Direct messages, a Public room everyone shares, and Groups you create yourself and invite people into.

Superuser tools

You're a superuser. The Admin Panel app lets you set anyone's balance, mute or deactivate accounts, and clear malware off someone's account directly — all without needing their password.
